Privacy Policy — Paddle Bounce

Effective Date: September 19, 2026

Paddle Bounce is a simple offline arcade game developed as an independent mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information.

1. Information We Collect

Paddle Bounce does not collect, request, or store personal information.

The app does not require users to create an account or provide information such as their name, email address, phone number, location, or other personal details.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Paddle Bounce may store your high score locally on your device so that your best score can be displayed when you return to the game.

This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to the developer or any external server.

3. Internet and Network Access

Paddle Bounce is designed to work offline and does not require an internet connection to play.

The app does not intentionally transmit gameplay data or personal information to external servers.

4. Third-Party Services

Paddle Bounce does not use third-party advertising services, analytics services, social media SDKs, or other third-party services that collect personal information.

5. Children's Privacy

Paddle Bounce does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

Because the app does not collect personal information, users can play without providing personal details.

6. Data Security

Paddle Bounce does not collect or transmit personal information to external servers.

Any locally stored game information, such as the player's high score, remains on the user's device.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the functionality of Paddle Bounce changes or if necessary to reflect applicable requirements.

Any updated version will replace the previous version.

8. Contact Us

Email: paddlebouncedevelopers@gmail.com

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Paddle Bounce, you may contact the developer using the contact information provided above or on the Google Play Store listing.

Paddle Bounce is designed as a simple, offline, privacy-friendly arcade game with no account required and no personal information collected.
